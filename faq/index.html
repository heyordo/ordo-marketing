Ordo FAQ

Direct answers about Ordo.

Ordo: Brain Dump is a free iPhone app for capturing mixed thoughts by typing or speaking. It separates a dump into individual items and sorts them into Now, Next, and Someday. No account is required.

What is a brain dump app?

A brain dump app gives mixed tasks, ideas, reminders, and loose thoughts one quick place to land before you organize them. Ordo separates a mixed dump into individual items and sorts them into Now, Next, and Someday.

Is Ordo free?

Yes. Ordo is free to download and use on iPhone.

Does Ordo need an account?

No. Ordo does not require an account, email address, or sign-in.

How does Ordo sort thoughts?

Ordo separates a dump into individual items, then sorts each item by when it appears to matter: Now for today, Next for soon, and Someday for ideas or things with no immediate rush. You can move or edit every result.

Does Ordo work offline?

If you're offline, Ordo saves your whole dump right away as one card on your iPhone and makes a quick on-device guess about where it belongs. When you're back online, Ordo splits it into individual cards and sorts them properly.

Can I talk to Ordo?

Yes. In version 1.4, talk for up to five minutes. Ordo keeps listening, then sorts everything into Now, Next, and Someday. Start from the app, a Home Screen widget with a mic, a Lock Screen widget, a Control Center control, the Action Button on iPhones that have one (iOS 18+), or Siri: “Talk to Ordo”.

Can Ordo remind me?

Yes. Pick a time, then choose Once, Until Done (it comes back until you tap Done), or an alarm on iOS 26. You can tap Done or Snooze right on the notification.

Is what I say stored?

Where supported, speech becomes text on your iPhone. Ordo does not receive or store your audio. The text—not the audio—is sent through Ordo's Cloudflare service to Anthropic for sorting. Ordo does not store dump text in a server-side content database; your saved cards stay on your iPhone. Anthropic's standard API retention can be up to 30 days, as explained in the privacy policy.

Is my data private?

Your saved items live on your iPhone. To sort a dump, its text is sent through Ordo's Cloudflare service to Anthropic and is not stored in a content database controlled by Ordo. Anthropic's standard API retention can be up to 30 days. Ordo has no accounts, ads in the app, or cross-app tracking. Ordo records at most one app open per install per day, and each sorted dump, as anonymous usage with a scrambled app-install ID, the date, and app version. The daily open record also says whether at least one reminder is set (yes or no; no reminder details) and whether notifications are allowed (yes or no; the iPhone setting only, no notification content). If a reminder is set or notifications are allowed later that day, the yes updates the same daily record. Sorted-dump counts also include whether you typed or spoke and the number of cards—never the dump text. Ordo asks Apple whether the install came from an Apple Search Ads ad and may retry until Apple provides an answer. Apple's response may contain other details, but Ordo keeps only the yes/no result, once per install, with the scrambled ID. No advertising identifier is used. These records are deleted in a daily cleanup once they are older than 90 days. Technical crash reports may be sent to Sentry; it is configured not to send default personal identifiers, and Ordo does not intentionally attach dump text. Read the full privacy policy.
