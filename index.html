What is Ordo?

A brain dump app for iPhone.

Ordo: Brain Dump is a free iPhone app for capturing mixed thoughts by typing or speaking. It separates a dump into individual items and sorts them into Now, Next, and Someday. No account is required.

Reminders, when you want them

A thought can stay a thought—or become a nudge.

  • Notification or alarm. “Remind me” sends a notification. “Wake me up” sets a real alarm that rings like the Clock app on iOS 26 and up.
  • Reminder settings. Choose the default type, snooze length, and whether notifications can break through Focus.
  • Show in Apple Reminders. Optionally let your reminders appear in Apple's Reminders app.
