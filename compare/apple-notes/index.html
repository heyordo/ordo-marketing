Ordo vs Apple Notes

Ordo is better at turning one messy capture into a short, sorted list. Apple Notes is better when the note itself is what you want to keep.

Updated October 4, 2026

Ordo: Brain Dump is a free iPhone app for capturing mixed thoughts by typing or speaking. It separates a dump into individual items and sorts them into Now, Next, and Someday. No account is required.

Choose Ordo when

You want to say or type several unrelated thoughts at once and have them separated and sorted automatically.

Choose Apple Notes when

You want to write or preserve a detailed note, scan documents, add attachments, draw, or collaborate.

The main difference

Apple Notes gives you a flexible blank page. Ordo gives you a temporary landing place for mixed thoughts, then turns that dump into individual cards organized by when they matter.

Where Apple Notes is better

Apple Notes is more capable for writing, reference material, checklists, document scans, attachments, folders, and shared notes. It is also a better choice when you need the entire workflow to remain available offline.

Where Ordo is better

Ordo removes the decision of where each thought belongs during capture. You can enter “call Maya, finish the report, trip idea, milk” as one dump and review separate cards in Now, Next, and Someday afterward.

Can they work together?

Yes. Use Ordo for fast capture and sorting. Move information worth keeping as reference into Apple Notes. The tools do not need to replace each other.
