Ordo vs a paper brain dump

Paper gives you complete freedom and never needs a connection. Ordo does more after the capture: it separates, sorts, and can remind you later.

Updated October 4, 2026

Ordo: Brain Dump is a free iPhone app for capturing mixed thoughts by typing or speaking. It separates a dump into individual items and sorts them into Now, Next, and Someday. No account is required.

Choose Ordo when

You want your dump separated into movable items, sorted automatically, and connected to reminders.

Choose paper when

You want zero data transmission, complete offline use, a free-form page, and no phone distraction.

The main difference

A paper brain dump ends as a page you review yourself. Ordo turns the dump into separate cards and proposes where each belongs: Now, Next, or Someday.

Where paper is better

Paper is private by default, fully offline, flexible, inexpensive, and free of notifications or other phone distractions. It is also better for diagrams, arrows, sketches, and spatial thinking.

Where Ordo is better

Ordo handles the review step. It separates one stream into individual cards, proposes when each matters, keeps the list with you, and can turn a time into a reminder or alarm.

Which should you use?

If writing by hand helps you think and you enjoy reviewing the page, use paper. If the paper tends to disappear or the sorting step stops you from starting, try Ordo. Both follow the same useful order: capture first, organize second.
